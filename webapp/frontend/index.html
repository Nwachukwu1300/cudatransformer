EXPERIMENT 04 DECODER-ONLY TRANSFORMER

What should you watch next?

I built the whole stack myself—the CUDA kernels, the autograd engine, the transformer, the tokenizer, the training loop. No PyTorch, no TensorFlow, no ML libraries of any kind. Then I pointed that same from-scratch model at a new problem: instead of predicting the next word in a sentence, predict the next movie you'd watch. It runs live below, on a CPU, in NumPy.
